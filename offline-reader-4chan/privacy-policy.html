Privacy Policy for Offline Reader for 4chan

Last updated 25 November 2026

Offline Reader for 4chan is made by MapleBase Technologies Inc., a company in Richmond Hill, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version is that it doesn't collect any.

The app is not made by 4chan and has no connection to it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, so we never ask for your name or email address. We don't run a server. The app has no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't send crash reports. Nothing about how you use the app reaches us or anyone else.

Who the app connects to

When you open or download a board or a thread, your phone connects straight to 4chan's servers to fetch the posts, images and videos. If you report a post, the app opens 4chan's own report page. Links inside posts open in your browser.

These are ordinary web requests. Like a browser, they show your IP address to the site you're contacting. 4chan handles that under its own rules, which are on its site at https://www.4chan.org

Buying Premium

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google takes the payment, and we never see your card details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium. Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy

What stays on your phone

Your downloads and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. You can delete any download from the Downloads screen, and uninstalling the app removes everything. An image or video only leaves that storage when you save it to a folder you pick, or share it.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy the app's settings to your Google account. Downloads are left out of that backup. We can't see it either way.

Permissions

The app needs internet access to download, and it checks whether you're on Wi-Fi or mobile data so it can ask before using mobile data. It asks for permission to show notifications so you can follow a download. Downloads run as a foreground service, which keeps them going when you switch to another app.

The thread watcher, which is off unless you turn it on, can keep the phone awake for a moment while it checks threads you bookmarked.

Adult content

Boards that 4chan marks as 18+ are hidden unless you turn them on in the app's settings. Turning them on asks you to confirm that you're 18 or older. That choice is saved only on your phone.

Children

The app isn't meant for anyone under 18. Content on 4chan isn't made for children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version at this address and update the date at the top.
