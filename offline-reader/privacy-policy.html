Privacy Policy: Offline Reader for Reddit

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Offline Reader for Reddit ("the app") is an app for reading Reddit offline. It is free to use, with an optional one-time Premium purchase. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Who we are

The app is developed and published by MapleBase Technologies Inc., a company incorporated in Canada (Richmond Hill, Ontario). "We" and "us" in this policy mean MapleBase Technologies Inc.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no login. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • The app doesn't collect, store or sell any personal data on any server of ours. We don't run a server.
  • Everything you download (posts, comments, images, videos, article copies) and your settings stay on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing about how you use the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the app connects to

To download a subreddit you ask for, the app sends requests directly from your phone to:

  • Reddit (reddit.com and its media hosts such as i.redd.it, v.redd.it, preview.redd.it), using the app's own Reddit API credentials, to fetch public posts, comments and media.
  • The websites posts link to (for example news sites, Imgur, Streamable, Giphy), to save the linked article, image or video for offline reading.

These requests are ordinary web requests: like any app or browser, they reveal your IP address and a user agent to the site you contact. Those services handle that information under their own privacy policies (for Reddit: https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy). The app doesn't send them anything about you beyond what a web request requires.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium, to unlock it on this device. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data on your device

  • Downloaded content and the list of subreddits you follow are stored in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete downloads at any time (pull down on a feed → *Manage…* → *Clear all*), and uninstalling the app deletes everything.
  • If you enable Android's backup service in the app's settings, Android may back up the app's settings to your own Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account; we can't access it.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to download content.
  • Notifications: to show download progress and let you cancel a download.
  • Foreground service (data sync): so a download you started keeps running if you leave the app.
  • Run at startup, vibrate, wake lock, sync settings, NFC (optional): features inherited from Reddit is Fun (scheduled cache cleanup, widgets, sharing). None of them collect data.

Adult content

Posts marked as adult (NSFW) on Reddit are filtered out, and adult subreddits can't be downloaded.

Children

The app shows content from Reddit, which is not directed at children. The app is not intended for users under 18.
