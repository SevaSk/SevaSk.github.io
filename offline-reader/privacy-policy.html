Privacy Policy for Offline Reader for Reddit

Last updated 19 October 2026

Offline Reader for Reddit is made by MapleBase Technologies Inc., a company in Richmond Hill, Ontario, Canada. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version is that it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, so we never ask for your name or email address. We don't run a server. The app has no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't send crash reports. Nothing about how you use the app reaches us or anyone else.

Who the app connects to

When you download a subreddit, your phone connects straight to Reddit to fetch the posts and their comments. It also connects to the sites those posts link to, like news sites or Imgur, so it can save the article or video for later.

These are ordinary web requests. Like a browser, they show your IP address to the site you're contacting. Reddit and those sites handle that under their own privacy policies. Reddit's is at https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy

Buying Premium

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google takes the payment, and we never see your card details. The app only asks Google Play whether your account owns Premium. Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy

What stays on your phone

Your downloads and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. You can delete any download from the download screen, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy the app's settings to your Google account. We can't see that backup.

Permissions

The app needs internet access to download, and permission to show notifications so you can follow a download. Downloads run as a foreground service, which keeps them going when you switch to another app.

A few other permissions come from Reddit is Fun, such as vibrate, wake lock, start at boot, sync settings and NFC. They are used for things like cleaning up old files and sharing links. None of them collect data.

Adult content

Posts and subreddits marked NSFW are hidden and won't download unless you turn on adult content in the app's settings. Turning it on asks you to confirm that you're 18 or older. That choice is saved only on your phone.

Children

The app isn't meant for anyone under 18. Content on Reddit isn't made for children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version at this address and update the date at the top.
